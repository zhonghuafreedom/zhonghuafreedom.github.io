本網站以公開資訊整理與人權倡議為目的。請不要透過公開表單提交可能危及本人或他人的敏感資料。如需聯繫，請先評估自身安全與所在地法律風險。

資料處理原則

  • 不主動收集敏感身份資料。

  • 外部表單或電郵服務可能有其自身政策。

This site is for public-interest documentation and human rights advocacy. Do not submit sensitive information that could endanger yourself or others through public forms.

Data principles

  • We do not intentionally collect sensitive identity data.
  • We do not publish non-consensual personal information.
  • External form or email providers may apply their own policies.
