2025年的中國抗議並非單一政治運動，而是由經濟壓力、地方治理、拖欠工資、房地產爛尾、土地徵收、校園事件與養老問題共同推動的分散行動。

工人：欠薪與工廠轉移

工人抗議通常圍繞拖欠工資、社保、公積金、工廠搬遷補償和裁員。地方政府在維穩邏輯下常以刪帖、約談、行政拘留或警力驅散回應，而非建立可預期的談判機制。

業主：爛尾樓與地方債務

房地產危機使業主和購房者成為重要抗議群體。當開發商資金鏈斷裂、樓盤停工、地方政府監管失靈時，受害者往往只能透過集體上訪、拉橫幅或網絡曝光爭取回應。

農村居民：土地、殯葬與基層治理

農村抗議涉及土地徵收、補償不公、地方工程、宗教與傳統習俗、殯葬政策等。這些事件表面上是地方矛盾，實際上反映了集體土地制度、地方財政壓力和缺乏獨立司法救濟的結構問題。

記錄原則

公開記錄抗議事件時，應避免暴露未公開的個人姓名、住址、聯絡方式或可識別影像。本頁以已公開報告、媒體與人權組織資料為基礎，並盡量標明時間、地點、訴求、當局回應與來源。

Protest in China in 2025 was not a single political movement. It was a dispersed pattern driven by economic pressure, local governance failure, wage arrears, unfinished housing, land takings, school incidents, and pension disputes.

Workers: unpaid wages and factory relocation

Worker actions often centered on wage arrears, social insurance, housing funds, relocation compensation, and layoffs. Local authorities commonly responded through censorship, summonses, administrative detention, or police dispersal rather than predictable negotiation channels.

Homeowners: unfinished housing and local debt

The real-estate crisis made homeowners and buyers a major protest constituency. When developers ran out of funds, projects stopped, and local regulators failed to intervene, victims often relied on collective petitions, banners, or online exposure.

Rural residents: land, funerals, and local governance

Rural protest involved land requisition, compensation, local construction projects, religious and customary practices, and funeral policy. These disputes point to structural problems: collective land arrangements, local fiscal pressure, and weak access to independent legal remedies.

Documentation principle

When documenting protest, do not expose private names, addresses, contact details, or identifiable images that are not already public. Prefer public reports, media, and rights-organization material, and record time, place, demand, official response, and source.
