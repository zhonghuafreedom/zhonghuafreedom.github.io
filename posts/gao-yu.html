資深記者高瑜案顯示，涉及黨內文件、新聞自由和普世價值的討論可被包裝成國家秘密案件。Veteran journalist Gao Yu’s case shows how discussion of party documents, press freedom, and universal values can be turned into a state-secrets prosecution.ベテラン記者高瑜の事件は、党文書、報道の自由、普遍的価値に関する議論が国家秘密事件にされ得ることを示します。원로 기자 가오위 사건은 당 문건, 언론 자유, 보편 가치 논의가 국가기밀 사건으로 바뀔 수 있음을 보여줍니다.El caso de la veterana periodista Gao Yu muestra cómo documentos del partido, libertad de prensa y valores universales pueden convertirse en una causa de secretos de Estado.Der Fall der erfahrenen Journalistin Gao Yu zeigt, wie Debatten über Parteidokumente, Pressefreiheit und universelle Werte zu Staatsgeheimnisverfahren werden können.Le cas de la journaliste vétérane Gao Yu montre comment documents du parti, liberté de la presse et valeurs universelles peuvent être transformés en affaire de secrets d’État.Saken mot veteran-journalisten Gao Yu viser hvordan partidokumenter, pressefrihet og universelle verdier kan gjøres til statshemmelighetssak.De zaak van veteraanjournalist Gao Yu toont hoe partijdocumenten, persvrijheid en universele waarden kunnen worden omgezet in een staatsgeheimenzaak.Il caso della veterana giornalista Gao Yu mostra come documenti del partito, libertà di stampa e valori universali possano diventare un processo per segreti di Stato.

高瑜是中國資深獨立記者。她因被指向境外媒體提供中共內部文件而被拘押，2015年被判七年，後改判五年並獲准保外就醫。其案是以國家秘密法處理新聞活動的代表案例。

時間線

  • 2014年，高瑜被拘押並在央視「認罪」畫面中出現。
  • 2015年4月，北京法院以「為境外非法提供國家秘密」判處她七年。
  • 2015年11月，二審改判五年，並允許保外就醫。
  • 國際新聞和人權組織長期認為此案與新聞自由和政治文件報導有關。

為何屬於言論自由迫害

高瑜案突出了「國家秘密」罪的彈性：黨內文件、政治路線和媒體討論若被視為秘密，調查報導就會被轉化為刑事風險。

現況與關注

截至2026年7月8日，高瑜並非以長期在押狀態作為本頁重點；重點在於其案件對新聞人、研究者和資料引用者的寒蟬效應。引用時應避免把被迫電視認罪當作自願陳述。

Gao Yu is a veteran independent Chinese journalist. She was detained after being accused of providing an internal party document to overseas media, sentenced to seven years in 2015, later reduced to five years and allowed medical parole. Her case is a leading example of using state-secrets law against journalism.

Timeline

  • In 2014, Gao was detained and appeared in a televised “confession.”
  • In April 2015, a Beijing court sentenced her to seven years for illegally providing state secrets overseas.
  • In November 2015, the sentence was reduced to five years and she was allowed medical parole.
  • International press and rights groups have long linked the case to press freedom and reporting on political documents.

Why this is free-expression persecution

Gao’s case shows the elasticity of the “state secrets” charge: party documents, political lines, and media discussion can be treated as secrets, turning reporting into criminal risk.

Status and concern

As of July 8, 2026, this page does not focus on Gao as a long-term current detainee; it focuses on the chilling effect her case has for journalists, researchers, and source citation. Televised confession material should not be treated as voluntary testimony.
