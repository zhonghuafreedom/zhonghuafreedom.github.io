方斌：武漢公民記錄者與秘密判刑Fang Bin: Wuhan Citizen Witness and Secret Sentencing方斌：武漢の市民記録者と秘密判決팡빈: 우한 시민 기록자와 비밀 판결Fang Bin: testigo ciudadano de Wuhan y sentencia secretaFang Bin: Bürgerzeuge aus Wuhan und geheimes UrteilFang Bin : témoin citoyen de Wuhan et condamnation secrèteFang Bin: borgerlig Wuhan-vitne og hemmelig domFang Bin: burgergetuige uit Wuhan en geheime veroordelingFang Bin: testimone cittadino di Wuhan e sentenza segreta

方斌因拍攝與發布武漢疫情初期影像而被拘押；其案顯示公民記錄如何被秘密審判與長期監控處理。Fang Bin was detained after filming and publishing scenes from early Wuhan; his case shows how citizen documentation can be handled through secret trial and continuing surveillance.方斌は武漢初期の映像を撮影・公開した後に拘束されました。市民記録が秘密裁判と監視で処理される事例です。팡빈은 우한 초기 상황을 촬영·게시한 뒤 구금되었습니다. 시민 기록이 비밀재판과 지속 감시로 처리되는 사례입니다.Fang Bin fue detenido tras filmar y publicar escenas del Wuhan inicial; su caso muestra juicio secreto y vigilancia posterior.Fang Bin wurde nach Aufnahmen aus dem frühen Wuhan festgenommen; sein Fall zeigt Geheimverfahren und fortgesetzte Überwachung.Fang Bin a été détenu après avoir filmé et publié des scènes du début de Wuhan; son cas montre procès secret et surveillance continue.Fang Bin ble pågrepet etter å ha filmet og publisert scener fra tidlig Wuhan; saken viser hemmelig rett og videre overvåking.Fang Bin werd vastgezet na beelden uit het vroege Wuhan; zijn zaak toont geheime berechting en voortdurende controle.Fang Bin fu detenuto dopo aver filmato e pubblicato scene della prima Wuhan; il caso mostra processo segreto e sorveglianza continua.

方斌是武漢服裝商人與公民記錄者。2020年疫情初期，他用手機拍攝武漢醫院與街頭狀況，並把影像發布到境外平台。此後他多次被警方帶走，最終長期失聯。

時間線

  • 2020年1月至2月，方斌發布武漢疫情影像。
  • 2020年2月，他因拍攝醫院外場景與抗議性標語後失聯。
  • RFA於2023年報導，他在秘密審判中被判三年以上，家屬未獲完整法律文件。
  • 2023年5月，媒體報導他刑滿釋放並被送回武漢。
  • 2024年，RFA報導他仍面臨居住與警察盤問等「維穩」壓力。

為何屬於言論自由迫害

方斌不是職業記者，但其行為屬於公民記錄與公共監督：拍攝、發布、質問疫情資訊管控。秘密審判與不公開判決使外界難以核查罪名、證據與程序，削弱了公正審判權。

現況與關注

即使獲釋，方斌仍可能受到限制居住、約談、驅趕與監控。記錄此案時，應把「刑滿釋放」與「恢復自由」分開，不應把出獄誤寫成不再受迫害。

Fang Bin is a Wuhan clothing seller and citizen witness. In early 2020 he filmed hospitals and streets in Wuhan and posted footage on overseas platforms. He was repeatedly taken by police and later disappeared for years.

Timeline

  • In January and February 2020, Fang published footage from Wuhan.
  • In February 2020, he disappeared after filming hospital scenes and protest language.
  • In 2023, RFA reported that he had been sentenced in a secret trial to more than three years, with no full legal documents provided to family.
  • In May 2023, media reported that he had been released and returned to Wuhan.
  • In 2024, RFA reported continued housing pressure and police questioning under “stability maintenance.”

Why this is free-expression persecution

Fang was not a professional reporter, but his conduct was citizen documentation and public oversight: filming, publishing, and questioning information control. Secret trial and opaque judgment made it difficult to verify charges, evidence, and procedure.

Status and concern

Release from custody should not be equated with restored freedom. Reports describe housing pressure, questioning, and surveillance after release. The site should record post-release restrictions separately from the end of a formal sentence.
