董瑤瓊是湖南籍女性。2018年7月4日，她在上海海航大廈外直播向習近平宣傳畫潑墨，並公開反對個人崇拜、思想控制與政治壓迫。此後她迅速失聯，家屬與人權團體稱她被送往湖南株洲精神病院。

時間線

  • 2018年7月4日，董瑤瓊直播潑墨抗議後失聯。
  • 2018年7月，RFA報導她被送往株洲市第三醫院接受強制「治療」。
  • 2020年1月，CHRD報導她從精神病院獲釋，但狀態明顯異常。
  • 2021年前後，外界再度失去她的可靠消息。
  • 2022年9月，她的父親董建彪在監獄中死亡，生前曾為女兒遭遇發聲。

為何屬於言論自由迫害

董瑤瓊並非因暴力行為受到正常司法審理，而是因公開政治表達被迅速失蹤、送醫與隔離。把反對領導人崇拜的抗議行為描述為精神問題，是把異議病理化、剝奪程序權利的典型模式。

Dong Yaoqiong is a woman from Hunan. On July 4, 2018, she livestreamed herself splashing ink on a Xi Jinping propaganda poster outside Shanghai’s HNA Building and denouncing personality cult, ideological control, and political repression. She soon disappeared; family members and rights groups reported that she was sent to a psychiatric hospital in Zhuzhou, Hunan.

Timeline

  • On July 4, 2018, Dong disappeared after the livestreamed ink protest.
  • In July 2018, RFA reported that she had been sent to Zhuzhou No. 3 Hospital for compulsory “treatment.”
  • In January 2020, CHRD reported that she had been released from psychiatric detention, but appeared changed.
  • Around 2021, reliable outside contact with her again disappeared.
  • In September 2022, her father Dong Jianbiao died in prison after speaking out about her case.

Why this is free-expression persecution

Dong was not given a transparent judicial process for ordinary public-order conduct. Her political expression was followed by disappearance, hospitalization, and isolation. Treating dissent against leader worship as a psychiatric problem pathologizes speech and strips away procedural protections.

Status and concern

As of July 8, 2026, her whereabouts and health remain unverified by independent public information. This site should distinguish confirmed facts, media reporting, and rights-group assessments, and avoid repeating unverified personal rumors.
