獨立記者黃雪琴與勞工權利倡議者王建兵因公民社會聚會與倡議，被以「煽動顛覆國家政權」判刑。Independent journalist Huang Xueqin and labour advocate Wang Jianbing were sentenced for “inciting subversion” after civil-society gatherings and advocacy.独立記者の黄雪琴と労働権活動家の王建兵は、市民社会の集まりと倡議を理由に「国家政権転覆扇動」で判決を受けました。독립기자 황쉐친과 노동권 활동가 왕젠빙은 시민사회 모임과 옹호 활동으로 “국가전복 선동” 판결을 받았습니다.La periodista independiente Huang Xueqin y el activista laboral Wang Jianbing fueron condenados por reuniones cívicas y defensa social.Die unabhängige Journalistin Huang Xueqin und der Arbeitsrechtsaktivist Wang Jianbing wurden wegen zivilgesellschaftlicher Treffen und Advocacy verurteilt.La journaliste indépendante Huang Xueqin et le défenseur des droits du travail Wang Jianbing ont été condamnés après des rencontres de société civile.Den uavhengige journalisten Huang Xueqin og arbeidsrettighetsforkjemper Wang Jianbing ble dømt etter sivilsamfunnsmøter og aktivisme.Onafhankelijk journalist Huang Xueqin en arbeidsrechtenactivist Wang Jianbing werden veroordeeld wegens civiele bijeenkomsten en belangenbehartiging.La giornalista indipendente Huang Xueqin e l’attivista del lavoro Wang Jianbing sono stati condannati per incontri civici e advocacy.

黃雪琴是獨立記者、女性權利與#MeToo倡議者；王建兵是勞工權利倡議者。二人於2021年9月19日被拘押，案件被國際組織視為中國打壓新聞、女性權利、勞工權利與公民結社空間的代表案例。

時間線

  • 2021年9月19日，黃雪琴與王建兵被廣州警方帶走。
  • 2023年9月22日，二人在廣州中級法院受審。
  • 2024年6月14日，黃雪琴被判五年，王建兵被判三年六個月。
  • 2024年9月，廣東省高院駁回上訴並維持原判。

為何屬於言論與結社迫害

公開資料顯示，當局將二人的倡議、聚會、討論中國公民社會困境、關注性別與勞工議題等活動納入「煽動顛覆國家政權」敘事。這不是普通刑事犯罪，而是把公共討論、互助網絡和社會倡議政治犯罪化。

現況與關注

黃雪琴的刑期因審前羈押計入而預計延續至2026年。王建兵的刑期與附加剝奪政治權利也應在記錄中分開標註。涉及二人朋友、支持者或聚會參與者時，網站不應公開未經授權的身份細節。

Huang Xueqin is an independent journalist and #MeToo advocate; Wang Jianbing is a labour-rights advocate. They were detained on September 19, 2021. International groups view the case as emblematic of China’s repression of journalism, women’s rights, labour advocacy, and civic association.

Timeline

  • On September 19, 2021, Huang and Wang were taken away by Guangzhou police.
  • On September 22, 2023, they were tried at the Guangzhou Intermediate People’s Court.
  • On June 14, 2024, Huang was sentenced to five years and Wang to three years and six months.
  • In September 2024, Guangdong’s high court rejected their appeal and upheld the verdict.

Why this is expression and association persecution

Public sources indicate that advocacy, gatherings, discussion of civil-society challenges, and work on gender and labour rights were folded into the charge of “inciting subversion of state power.” The case criminalizes public discussion, mutual support networks, and social advocacy.

Status and concern

Huang’s sentence, counting pretrial detention, extends into 2026. Wang’s prison term and the additional deprivation of political rights should be recorded separately. The site should not publish identifying details of friends, supporters, or participants without authorization.
