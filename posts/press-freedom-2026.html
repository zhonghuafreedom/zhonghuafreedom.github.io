新聞自由是衡量政治開放度的核心指標。中國大陸與香港的共同趨勢，是把新聞工作重新定義為「國安風險」或「境外勾連」，讓獨立報導承擔刑事後果。

為什麼RSF與CPJ數字不同

RSF與CPJ使用不同統計口徑。RSF頁面顯示即時拘押記者與媒體工作者；CPJ監獄普查是每年12月1日的快照，且只計入CPJ確認與新聞工作直接相關的案件。兩者不是互相否定，而是從不同角度說明同一問題：新聞工作在中國承擔高度刑事風險。

香港：從新聞中心到國安案件場域

《蘋果日報》《立場新聞》等媒體停運後，香港記者協會、獨立記者和流亡媒體仍面對法律、財務、簽證、採訪與人身安全壓力。Jimmy Lai案則成為香港國安法如何處理媒體與政治表達的標誌性案件。

Press freedom is a core indicator of political openness. In mainland China and Hong Kong, independent reporting is increasingly treated as a national-security risk or foreign-collusion issue, exposing journalists and editors to criminal punishment.

178/180China’s 2026 RSF World Press Freedom Index rank
110+3journalists and media workers detained on RSF’s China page

7of CPJ’s China cases were in Hong Kong

Why RSF and CPJ counts differ

RSF and CPJ use different methodologies. RSF displays current detained journalists and media workers; CPJ’s prison census is an annual snapshot taken on December 1 and includes cases CPJ has confirmed as work-related. The numbers are not contradictory; they show different views of the same risk environment.

Hong Kong: from media hub to national-security case file

After the closure of Apple Daily, Stand News, and other outlets, independent reporters, the Hong Kong Journalists Association, and exile media continue to face legal, financial, visa, access, and safety pressures. The Jimmy Lai case has become a symbol of how Hong Kong’s national-security regime treats media and political expression.

Website recommendation

The site can maintain a “detained journalists and media cases” table with name, role, outlet, date of detention, charge, sentence/status, and source. Avoid publishing family details or nonpublic identifiers.
