李澤華追蹤武漢疫情和陳秋實失聯事件後被帶走，後以「隔離」名義重新露面。Li Zehua was taken away after reporting on Wuhan and Chen Qiushi’s disappearance, then resurfaced under a “quarantine” explanation.李澤華は武漢疫情と陳秋実失踪を追った後に連行され、「隔離」という説明で再登場しました。리쩌화는 우한 상황과 천추스 실종을 취재한 뒤 연행됐고, 이후 “격리” 설명과 함께 다시 나타났습니다。Li Zehua fue llevado tras informar sobre Wuhan y la desaparición de Chen Qiushi, y luego reapareció bajo una explicación de “cuarentena”.Li Zehua wurde nach Berichten über Wuhan und Chen Qiushis Verschwinden weggebracht und tauchte später mit der Erklärung “Quarantäne” wieder auf.Li Zehua a été emmené après avoir enquêté sur Wuhan et la disparition de Chen Qiushi, puis a réapparu sous l’explication de la “quarantaine”.Li Zehua ble tatt bort etter rapportering om Wuhan og Chen Qiushis forsvinning, og dukket senere opp med forklaringen “karantene”.Li Zehua werd meegenomen na berichtgeving over Wuhan en de verdwijning van Chen Qiushi, en verscheen later met de uitleg “quarantaine”.Li Zehua fu portato via dopo aver riferito su Wuhan e sulla scomparsa di Chen Qiushi, poi riapparve con la spiegazione della “quarantena”.

李澤華曾任中國央視主持人，2020年2月前往武漢進行獨立拍攝。他追蹤陳秋實失聯事件，並發布火葬場、社區與警察追車等內容，隨後在直播中失去自由。

時間線

  • 2020年2月，李澤華抵達武漢並開始發布獨立視頻。
  • 2020年2月26日，他在直播中稱警察追蹤其車輛，隨後被帶走。
  • 2020年4月，他發布視頻稱自己被隔離並接受調查。
  • CPJ後來報導，李澤華離開中國後表示當時的「返回」視頻帶有壓力背景。

為何屬於言論自由迫害

李澤華以新聞從業背景進行公共記錄，內容涉及疫情處置、失聯記者和地方管控。以「隔離」方式中止報導，並要求其按官方敘事回應，削弱了獨立採訪權。

現況與關注

截至2026年7月8日，公開資料顯示他已離開中國並談及當年遭遇。此案應作為疫情信息封鎖和公民記錄風險的案例，而非單純的防疫隔離事件。

Li Zehua, a former CCTV presenter, went to Wuhan in February 2020 to conduct independent reporting. He followed Chen Qiushi’s disappearance and posted videos about funeral homes, communities, and police pursuit before losing freedom during a livestream.

Timeline

  • In February 2020, Li arrived in Wuhan and began posting independent videos.
  • On February 26, 2020, he said during a livestream that police were following his car and was then taken away.
  • In April 2020, he posted a video saying he had been quarantined and investigated.
  • CPJ later reported that after leaving China, Li described the “return” video as made under pressure.

Why this is free-expression persecution

Li used a professional media background to document matters of public interest: pandemic response, missing reporters, and local control. Ending that reporting through “quarantine” and pressuring him into an official narrative undermined independent newsgathering.

Status and concern

As of July 8, 2026, public reporting indicates that Li left China and discussed what happened. The case should be recorded as pandemic information control and a warning to citizen documentation, not merely as a quarantine episode.
