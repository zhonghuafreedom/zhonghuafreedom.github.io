陳秋實赴武漢發布非官方影像後失聯，其案顯示「隔離」「監控」可被用來消除公共報導。After publishing unofficial reporting from Wuhan, Chen Qiushi disappeared from public view, showing how “quarantine” and surveillance can erase public reporting.武漢から非公式映像を発信した後に姿を消した陳秋実の事例は、「隔離」と監視が報道を消す手段となることを示します。우한에서 비공식 영상을 게시한 뒤 사라진 천추스 사건은 “격리”와 감시가 공공 보도를 지우는 수단이 될 수 있음을 보여줍니다.Tras publicar reportajes no oficiales desde Wuhan, Chen Qiushi desapareció de la vista pública; el caso muestra cómo la “cuarentena” y la vigilancia pueden borrar la información pública.Nach inoffiziellen Berichten aus Wuhan verschwand Chen Qiushi aus der Öffentlichkeit; sein Fall zeigt, wie “Quarantäne” und Überwachung Berichterstattung auslöschen können.Après avoir publié des reportages non officiels depuis Wuhan, Chen Qiushi a disparu de l’espace public; son cas montre comment “quarantaine” et surveillance peuvent effacer l’information.Etter uoffisiell rapportering fra Wuhan forsvant Chen Qiushi fra offentligheten; saken viser hvordan “karantene” og overvåking kan fjerne offentlig rapportering.Na onofficiële verslaggeving vanuit Wuhan verdween Chen Qiushi uit beeld; de zaak toont hoe “quarantaine” en toezicht publieke berichtgeving kunnen wissen.Dopo aver pubblicato reportage non ufficiali da Wuhan, Chen Qiushi è scomparso dalla scena pubblica; il caso mostra come “quarantena” e sorveglianza possano cancellare l’informazione.

陳秋實是前律師、視頻博主和公民記者。2020年1月，他前往武漢拍攝醫院、居民與封城狀況，並把影像發布到境外平台。2020年2月失聯後，外界長期無法核實其法律狀態。

時間線

  • 2020年1月下旬，陳秋實抵達武漢並發布多段現場視頻。
  • 2020年2月6日前後，他與外界失去聯絡，朋友稱其被帶走或被隔離。
  • CPJ與HRW隨後呼籲釋放包括陳秋實在內的疫情公民記者。
  • 2021年他曾重新露面，但未能公開說明失聯期間的完整細節。

為何屬於言論自由迫害

陳秋實的核心行為是拍攝、訪問與發布非官方疫情資訊。當公共衛生危機中的記錄被迫中止，且當事人法律狀態長期不透明，這本身就是對新聞自由和公民表達的壓制。

現況與關注

截至2026年7月8日，公開資料未顯示陳秋實曾經接受透明審判或獲得充分程序說明。網站記錄此案時應避免把重新露面等同於完全自由，並區分官方說法、友人說法和國際組織報告。

Chen Qiushi is a former lawyer, video blogger, and citizen journalist. In January 2020 he traveled to Wuhan to film hospitals, residents, and lockdown conditions, then posted the footage on overseas platforms. After he lost contact in February 2020, his legal status remained opaque for a long period.

Timeline

  • In late January 2020, Chen arrived in Wuhan and posted multiple on-the-ground videos.
  • Around February 6, 2020, he lost contact; friends said he had been taken away or placed under quarantine.
  • CPJ and HRW later called for the release of COVID citizen journalists including Chen.
  • He reappeared publicly in 2021, but could not give a full public account of the period of disappearance.

Why this is free-expression persecution

Chen’s conduct consisted of filming, interviewing, and publishing unofficial information during a public-health crisis. When documentation is forcibly interrupted and legal status remains opaque, the effect is suppression of press freedom and citizen expression.

Status and concern

As of July 8, 2026, public sources do not show that Chen received a transparent trial or full procedural explanation. This site should avoid treating reappearance as full freedom and should separate official accounts, friends’ accounts, and rights-group reporting.
