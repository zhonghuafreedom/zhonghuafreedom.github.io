端點星（Terminus2049）是一個在網絡上備份被刪除公共文章、疫情報導和個人記錄的項目。陳玫、蔡偉等志願者因保存被審查內容而被拘押，案件凸顯「保存記憶」也會被刑事化。

時間線

  • 2018年前後，端點星項目開始在GitHub等平台備份被刪內容。
  • 2020年疫情期間，項目保存多篇被刪報導、訪談和個人敘述。
  • 2020年4月，陳玫、蔡偉等人失聯並被拘押。
  • 2021年，陳玫與蔡偉被判刑，隨後因羈押期抵刑獲釋。

為何屬於言論自由迫害

端點星不是暴力組織，而是保存公共資料的志願項目。以刑事罪名處理資料備份，等於把資訊記憶、研究引用和反審查行為置於刑事風險之中。

現況與關注

截至2026年7月8日，兩人已據公開資料獲釋，但案件仍是中國網絡審查和疫情記憶清除的重要案例。網站引用時應強調公開資料、非暴力記錄和司法程序問題。

Terminus2049 was an online project that backed up deleted public articles, pandemic reporting, and personal records. Volunteers including Chen Mei and Cai Wei were detained for preserving censored material, showing that “saving memory” can itself be criminalized.

Timeline

  • Around 2018, Terminus2049 began backing up deleted material on platforms such as GitHub.
  • During the 2020 pandemic, the project preserved deleted reporting, interviews, and personal narratives.
  • In April 2020, Chen Mei, Cai Wei, and others lost contact and were detained.
  • In 2021, Chen and Cai were sentenced and later released because time already served counted toward the sentence.

Why this is free-expression persecution

Terminus2049 was not a violent group; it was a volunteer archive of public information. Criminalizing data preservation turns memory, research citation, and anti-censorship work into criminal risk.

Status and concern

As of July 8, 2026, public sources indicate that both were released, but the case remains a major example of Chinese internet censorship and erasure of pandemic memory. This site should emphasize public material, nonviolent archiving, and procedural concerns.
