跨境鎮壓是威權政府把國內控制延伸到海外的方式。它不只針對名人或流亡領袖，也會影響留學生、家屬、社群組織、研究者、記者與地方政治人物。

常見工具

  • 對海外人士本人進行跟蹤、威脅、肢體攻擊或網絡騷擾。
  • 對其在中國境內家屬施壓，要求停止活動或刪除言論。
  • 以懸賞、通緝、護照與簽證問題製造恐懼。
  • 透過法律戰、誹謗訴訟、學術與商業壓力打擊批評者。
  • 使用社交媒體假帳號、性化騷擾、AI生成內容或舉報機制進行羞辱與噤聲。

主要目標群體

公開報告反覆提到的目標包括海外港人、維吾爾人、藏人、法輪功修煉者、中國民主活動人士、人權律師、前中國官員、留學生與研究中國議題的學者。

記錄建議

網站可以新增「跨境鎮壓案例庫」，但需保護當事人安全。建議只收錄已公開案例，避免公布未授權姓名、地址、親屬資訊或移民狀態。每個案例可記錄：日期、地點、手段、受害群體、公開來源、當事人是否已公開發聲。

安全提醒

遭遇威脅者應保留截圖、郵件頭、電話記錄和事件時間線，並考慮聯繫所在地執法機關、學校、媒體組織或人權團體。網站不應要求受害者公開提交敏感材料。

Transnational repression is the extension of domestic authoritarian control into other countries. It affects not only well-known exiles, but also students, relatives, community groups, researchers, journalists, and local politicians.

Common tools

  • Surveillance, threats, physical assault, or online harassment of people abroad.
  • Pressure on relatives inside China to force silence overseas.
  • Bounties, arrest warrants, passport or visa pressure, and fear-inducing notices.
  • Lawfare, defamation suits, academic pressure, and business pressure against critics.
  • Fake social-media accounts, sexualized harassment, AI-generated content, and reporting mechanisms used to shame or silence targets.

Main target groups

Public reports repeatedly identify overseas Hongkongers, Uyghurs, Tibetans, Falun Gong practitioners, Chinese democracy activists, human rights lawyers, former Chinese officials, students, and scholars working on China-related topics.

Documentation guidance

The site can maintain a transnational repression case library, but safety comes first. Include only public cases unless explicit consent exists. Avoid publishing addresses, relatives, immigration status, or nonpublic identifiers. Record date, place, method, target group, public source, and whether the person has already spoken publicly.

Safety note

People facing threats should preserve screenshots, email headers, phone records, and a timeline of incidents, then consider contacting local law enforcement, their school or employer, journalists’ groups, or human rights organizations. The site should not ask victims to submit sensitive evidence publicly.
