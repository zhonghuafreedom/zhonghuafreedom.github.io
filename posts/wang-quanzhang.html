Event or topic overview

Wang Quanzhang is a lawyer who represents Falun Gong, land and other human rights cases. After he was taken away in 2015, his family and entrusted lawyers were unable to have normal contact with him for a long time. The RSDL caused the detention location and procedural information to be controlled by security agencies.

Detailed timeline and key developments

  1. 2015-07-10： Wang Quanzhang was taken away during Operation 709, and his family was unable to confirm the place of detention for a long time.
  2. 2015-08： The police placed Wang Quanzhang under residential surveillance at a designated residence, and his family and lawyers were unable to meet him normally.
  3. 2018-12-26： Tianjin No. 2 Intermediate People's Court does not hear Wang Quanzhang's case in public, and access to trial information to outsiders and family members is restricted.
  4. 2019-01-28： The court sentenced Wang Quanzhang to four years and six months for subverting state power.
  5. 2020-04-05： Wang Quanzhang was sent to Shandong for isolation after serving his sentence, and his wife Li Wenzu reported that she could not reunite with him immediately.
  6. 2020-04-27： Wang Quanzhang was still closely monitored after returning to Beijing, and Amnesty pointed out that his "freedom" was subject to restrictions on residence and movement.
  7. 2021-2024： Materials showing that Li Wenzu and his children were followed, expelled, and pressured to attend school show that state control extends to family life.

Key evidence and information control

The court ruling can confirm the trial in 2018 and the four-year and six-month sentence in 2019; Amnesty, family members and human rights organizations document the long-term isolation, the search operation for Li Wenzu, post-incarceration isolation and the pressure on their children's education.

Official position, silence and information control

The public security organs have limited public information on Wang Quanzhang’s disappearance and residential surveillance at a designated location. The Tianjin Second Intermediate Court announced a verdict in 2019; these materials can prove the official conviction and sentence, but do not explain the secret detention location, the selection of lawyers, or the specific reasons for not making the trial public. Records of family members being evicted, children's education affected, and post-incarceration surveillance show that information and life restrictions did not end with the sentence.

Advocacy pressure and state machinery

The public security, national security, detention center and Tianjin court successively controlled Wang Quanzhang’s detention and trial; after serving his sentence, local officials affected his family by imposing quarantine, residence and travel restrictions. Li Wenzu publicly searched for his husband and took his children to school, making the family itself the target of surveillance and obstruction.

Human rights and rule-of-law analysis

Lawyer Wang Quanzhang disappeared into residential surveillance at a designated location during the 709 crackdown, was later tried and sentenced, and after release continued to face controls affecting residence, movement, and family life with his wife Li Wenzu and their child. Public-security, state-security, detention, court, and local administrative powers combined secret custody, restricted counsel, closed proceedings, eviction or relocation pressure, and interference with a child’s education, extending punishment from legal work to an entire household. Authorities must disclose surveillance and interrogation records, investigate abuse independently, remove post-sentence controls, repair family and education harm, protect legal practice, and provide an effective remedy.

Relevance to protection review

Representation of sensitive clients, links to the 709 lawyers, contact with Wang Quanzhang or Li Wenzu, family advocacy, housing arrangements, school records, and public documentation of surveillance create identifiable professional and household trails. Secret custody, court control, local monitoring, travel restriction, housing pressure, and obstruction of a child’s education demonstrate capacity to continue punishment through family life after a sentence. Return, renewed legal work, or overseas support may sustain attention; the state must end associative and post-sentence retaliation, protect residence, education, counsel, and communication, disclose decisions, and provide independent relief.

Unresolved questions

What remains to be disclosed are: the location of designated residential surveillance, authorization and interrogation records; the reasons for not making the trial and selection of lawyers public; complete evidence and judgment materials; responsibilities for family members being evicted, children’s education being hindered and post-incarceration monitoring; and the judicial and administrative remedies available to Wang Quanzhang and Li Wenzu.

裁判所の判決により、2018年の裁判と2019年の懲役4年6月が確定する可能性がある。アムネスティ、家族、人権団体は、長期にわたる隔離、李文祖の捜索活動、投獄後の隔離、子どもたちの教育への圧力などを記録している。

公式見解、沈黙と情報統制

公安機関は、王全章氏の失踪と指定場所での居住監視に関する公開情報を制限している。天津第二中級裁判所は2019年に判決を発表した。これらの資料は正式な有罪判決と判決を証明することはできるが、秘密拘留の場所、弁護士の選定、裁判を公開しない具体的な理由については説明していない。家族の立ち退き、子どもの教育への影響、投獄後の監視の記録は、情報と生活の制限が判決で終わったわけではないことを示している。

権利擁護への圧力と国家機構の介入

公安、国家安全、拘置所、天津の裁判所は、王全章の拘禁と裁判を継続的に管理した。刑期を終えた後、地方当局は検疫、居住、渡航制限を課すことで彼の家族に影響を与えた。李文祖さんは公の場で夫を捜索し、子どもたちを学校に連れて行き、家族そのものが監視と妨害の対象となった。

人権・法の支配の分析

弁護士の王泉章氏は、709の弾圧中に指定された場所で住宅監視の中に姿を消し、後に裁判を受けて判決を受け、釈放後も住居、移動、妻の李文祖とその子供との家族生活に影響を与える規制に直面し続けた。公安、国家安全保障、拘留、裁判所、地方行政権限が秘密監護、弁護士の制限、非公開訴訟、立ち退きや転居の圧力、子供の教育への干渉を組み合わせ、法的手続きから世帯全体にまで刑罰を拡大した。当局は監視と尋問の記録を公開し、虐待を独自に調査し、判決後の管理を解除し、家族と教育への被害を修復し、法的慣行を保護し、効果的な救済策を提供しなければならない。

保護審査との関連

機密性の高いクライアントの代理、709 人の弁護士とのつながり、王全章や李文祖との接触、家族の擁護、住居の取り決め、学校の記録、監視に関する公的文書などにより、特定可能な職業的痕跡と家庭の痕跡が作成されます。極秘監護、法廷管理、地域監視、渡航制限、住居への圧力、子供の教育妨害は、判決後も家庭生活を通して刑罰を継続する能力を示している。復帰、法務活動の再開、または海外でのサポートが引き続き注目を集める可能性があります。国は連想的および判決後の報復をやめ、住居、教育、弁護士、コミュニケーションを保護し、決定を開示して独立した救済を提供しなければならない。

Wang Quanzhang es un abogado que representa casos de Falun Gong, tierras y otros derechos humanos.
