事件／專題概述

2022年10月13日，彭立發在北京四通橋懸掛反對封控與政治控制的橫幅並使用擴音設備。

現場影像和海外保存使事件得以傳播，但也使其公開身份與數位足跡高度可見。

詳細時間線或關鍵發展

  1. 2022-10-13： 彭立發在北京四通橋展示兩幅標語並使用擴音器，影像顯示警察其後到場。
  2. 2022-10-13： 他在現場被帶走；此後親友和公眾長期難以取得其拘押和程序的可靠公開資訊。
  3. 2022-10-14：中國網路平台迅速刪除相關圖片、影片及「四通橋」關鍵詞，海外使用者開始保存、備份和鏡像這些材料。
  4. 2022-10-18： 與抗議相關的網路帳號和討論遭限制，公開聲援者面臨被辨識和問話的風險。
  5. 2023-10-13： 海外多地舉行一週年紀念，彭立發的「Bridge Man」形象成為跨境政治表達的符號。
  6. 2025-07-08： CHRD依據其取得的案件資訊報道，彭立發被以煽動顛覆國家政權罪判處九年徒刑。

Event or topic overview

On October 13, 2022, Peng Lifa hung banners opposing blockades and political control on Beijing's Sitong Bridge and used loudspeaker equipment.

Live footage and preservation of evidence and records outside China enabled the event to be disseminated, but also made its public identity and digital footprint highly visible.

Detailed timeline and key developments

  1. 2022-10-13： Peng Lifa displayed two slogans and used a loudspeaker at Beijing's Sitong Bridge, and images showed police arriving later.
  2. 2022-10-13： He was taken away at the scene; family members, friends and the public have since struggled to obtain reliable public information about his detention and proceedings.
  3. 2022-10-14：Chinese online platforms rapidly removed related images, video, and the keyword “Sitong Bridge”; users abroad began preserving, backing up, and mirroring the material.
  4. 2022-10-18： Online accounts and discussions related to the protests have been restricted, and public supporters face the risk of being identified and questioned.
  5. 2023-10-13： The first anniversary was held in many places overseas, and Peng Lifa's "Bridge Man" image became a symbol of cross-border political expression.
  6. 2025-07-08: Citing case information it had obtained, CHRD reported that Peng Lifa had been sentenced to nine years in prison for ‘inciting subversion of state power.’

Key evidence and information control

Photos and videos from the scene support the protest date, slogans, and police presence; researchers and media documented deletion and keyword blocking on online platforms.

Citing case information it had obtained, CHRD reported in 2025 that Peng Lifa had been sentenced to nine years in prison for ‘inciting subversion of state power.’

Beijing police and online-information authorities have not disclosed where Peng Lifa was taken after displaying a banner at Sitong Bridge, the applicable legal procedure, or the case documents; platforms rapidly removed images and keywords. The official silence leaves unanswered who ordered the removal, whether lawyers and relatives received access, and whether charges and a judgment were issued. Deletion and risks to people who repost material make preservation and verification difficult for journalists, supporters, and family members.

Advocacy pressure and state machinery

Beijing police took Peng Lifa away at the scene, and there was a lack of publicly confirmed procedural information for a long time.

The platform's cleansing of searches and content on "Sitong Bridge" and "Peng Zaizhou", combined with questioning or pressure on relevant individuals, has weakened the ability of family members and the public to obtain information.

Human rights and rule-of-law analysis

On 13 October 2022, Peng Lifa hung banners and used a loudspeaker on Beijing’s Sitong Bridge to oppose lockdown policy and political control; police took him away at the scene, and platforms rapidly removed related images, keywords, and discussion. In 2025 CHRD reported, on the basis of case information it had obtained, that Peng Lifa was sentenced to nine years for inciting subversion of state power. Removal from the scene, prolonged secrecy about his place of custody and procedure, severe punishment for nonviolent expression, and coordinated erasure of the record violated his and his family’s rights to liberty, information, defence, expression, and an effective legal remedy. Beijing authorities must publish the removal, custody, charge, trial, and full judgment records, disclose censorship instructions, protect access to counsel and family, independently review the sentence, and provide an independent and effective legal remedy.

Relevance to protection review

Displaying the Sitong Bridge slogans, using or sharing the names Peng Lifa or Peng Zaizhou, preserving photographs from the scene, joining online commemorations, assisting his family, or circulating related protest material creates image, account, and communication records identifiable to police, cyberspace authorities, and platforms. His removal by police, prolonged secrecy about custody and proceedings, deletion of keywords and discussion, CHRD’s report of a nine-year sentence, and restricted family or lawyer access show that state bodies can extend a brief public expression into prolonged custody, digital monitoring, and family pressure. Return to China, anniversary commemoration, preservation abroad, or continued circulation may expose the person, other speakers, and relatives to renewed identification and reprisal. The Chinese government must stop retaliation, disclose custody status, case evidence, and the judgment, protect communication and access to counsel, end censorship, and provide an independent and effective legal remedy.

Unresolved questions

Still undisclosed are Peng Lifa’s place of custody, legal basis, and procedure after police took him away; whether lawyers and relatives can communicate with or visit him; the indictment, trial procedure, full judgment, and any appeal; the instructions to delete Sitong Bridge images, keywords, and discussion; the responsibility of the public-security and cyberspace bodies involved; and safe, independent complaint and legal-remedy routes available to the family.
