事件概述

“小洛熙”为一名五个月大女婴。宁波官方通报称，她于2025年11月14日在宁波大学附属妇女儿童医院手术后死亡，并于2026年2月被认定为一级甲等医疗事故。家属通过诉讼、公开视频和线下维权持续追问诊疗、病历、手术经过与责任；这些当事人材料、现场声援记录和海外传播共同保存了事件的完整脉络。

详细时间线

  • 2025年11月14日：小洛熙在宁波大学附属妇女儿童医院手术后死亡；家属随后持续要求病历、手术说明和责任回应。
  • 2026年1月12日：官方称相关专家回避，九名专家在公证及地方人大、政协代表监督下组成鉴定组。
  • 2026年2月5日：宁波通报认定一级甲等医疗事故，承认医院在医疗质量安全、风险防范、应急处置和医患沟通方面存在问题，并公布处理。
  • 2026年6月：家属公开视频披露，父母和祖母被要求前往奉化方桥派出所；声援者的现场记录及海外报道保存了聚集、警方维稳、劝离或送返声援者的经过。

关键证据与信息控制

家属公开视频、公开陈述、诉讼与线下维权资料，连同声援者的现场记录、锐报和新唐人等海外报道，共同构成理解事件经过的重要证据链。在删帖、限流、账号限制和维稳环境下，民间向海外保存和传播的材料尤具证据价值。地方部门未就家属前往方桥派出所后的具体处置、声援者受限的法律依据及相关传播限制作出充分公开说明。

维权压力与国家机器介入

医疗事故认定已指向医院在管理、风险防范、应急处置和医患沟通上的制度性问题；它不是对完整事实、责任与救济的终点。家属继续要求真相和责任时，派出所介入、声援者受限及传播空间收缩，使本应通过透明调查、医疗问责与司法救济处理的争议被纳入维稳框架，压缩了家属、声援者和传播者的表达空间。

Incident overview

"Xiao Luoxi" is a five-month-old baby girl. Ningbo official notification stated that she died after surgery at the Women and Children's Hospital Affiliated to Ningbo University on November 14, 2025, and was recognized as a Grade I Class A medical accident in February 2026. The family members continue to pursue the diagnosis and treatment, medical records, surgical procedures and responsibilities through litigation, public videos and in-person advocacy; these materials from those directly affected, on-site support records and overseas dissemination together preserve the complete context of the incident.

Detailed timeline

  • November 14, 2025: Little Luoxi died after surgery at the Women and Children’s Hospital of Ningbo University; her family members subsequently continued to demand medical records, surgical instructions and responsibility responses.
  • January 12, 2026: Officially, the relevant experts recused themselves, and nine experts formed an appraisal team under the supervision of notarization and representatives of the local People’s Congress and the Chinese People’s Political Consultative Conference.
  • February 5, 2026: Ningbo notified and recognized Class A medical accidents, admitted that the hospital had problems in medical quality and safety, risk prevention, emergency response and doctor-patient communication, and announced the solutions.
  • June 2026: Family members’ public video disclosed that parents and grandmother were asked to go to Fenghua Fangqiao Police Station; supporters’ on-site records and overseas reports preserved the process of gathering, police maintaining stability, and persuading supporters to leave or sending them away.

Key evidence and information control

Family members’ public videos, public statements, lawsuits and in-person advocacy materials, together with on-site records of supporters, overseas reports such as Ruibao and NTDTV, together form an important evidence chain for understanding the incident. In an environment of deletion of posts, reduced online visibility, account restrictions, and stability maintenance, materials preserved and disseminated overseas by private individuals are particularly valuable as evidence. The local department did not provide a full public explanation on the specific handling of the family members after they went to Fangqiao Police Station, the legal basis for the restriction of supporters, and related communication restrictions.

Pressure on advocacy and state intervention

The recognition of medical accidents has pointed to institutional problems in hospital management, risk prevention, emergency response and doctor-patient communication; it is not the end of complete facts, responsibility and relief. When the family members continue to demand the truth and responsibility, the intervention of the police station, the restriction of supporters, and the shrinking of communication space have caused disputes that should have been handled through transparent investigations, medical accountability, and judicial relief to be included in the stability maintenance framework, compressing the expression space of family members, supporters, and communicators.

Human rights and rule-of-law analysis

Five-month-old girl Xiao Luoxi died after surgery at the Women and Children’s Hospital Affiliated to Ningbo University; even after the case was classified as a Grade I Class A medical accident, her family still lacked a transparent account of the records, operative decisions, and chain of responsibility. When the family pursued litigation, videos, and public statements, the Fangqiao Police Station episode, restrictions on in-person solidarity, removal of online posts, and obstacles to preserving evidence added a second injury of police-led stability control and information suppression to the unresolved medical harm. The hospital, health authorities, and police must disclose verifiable records, accept an independent investigation, stop suppressing the family and supporters, and secure access to litigation, peaceful expression, evidence preservation, and an effective remedy.

Relevance to protection review

The family’s role in litigation, its public videos, contacts with supporters, records of the Fangqiao Police Station episode, and the digital trail created when material was preserved by overseas media and archivists are all concrete routes through which further pressure could be applied. Police capacity to control a gathering and identify participants, combined with platforms’ capacity to delete or reduce the visibility of posts, has already shown how pressure can reach relatives, supporters, and people who circulate the record. Where no safe complaint channel, independent medical inquiry, or unimpeded judicial remedy exists, silence cannot establish that danger has ended; the state remains responsible for preventing reprisals, protecting family contact and public speech, and ensuring that return or continued circulation of the case record does not trigger police or digital tracking.
