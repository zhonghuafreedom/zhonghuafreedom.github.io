2022年11月烏魯木齊火災後，多地出現反對清零政策並要求表達自由的集會。舉起空白紙張成為在審查環境下表達哀悼和訴求的符號。 After the November 2022 Urumqi fire, demonstrations against the zero-COVID policy and for freedom of expression took place in many places. Holding up blank sheets of paper became a symbol of mourning and expression under censorship. 2022年11月のウルムチ火災後、ゼロコロナ政策への抗議と表現の自由を求める集会が各地で行われた。白紙を掲げることは、検閲下で追悼と訴えを表す象徴となった。 2022년 11월 우루무치 화재 뒤 제로 코로나 정책에 반대하고 표현의 자유를 요구하는 집회가 여러 곳에서 열렸다. 빈 종이를 드는 행동은 검열 아래에서 애도와 요구를 표현하는 상징이 되었다. Tras el incendio de Urumqi de noviembre de 2022, hubo manifestaciones contra la política de cero COVID y a favor de la libertad de expresión. Alzar hojas en blanco se convirtió en un símbolo de duelo y expresión bajo la censura. Etter brannen i Urumqi i november 2022 ble det holdt demonstrasjoner mange steder mot null-covid-politikken og for ytringsfrihet. Å holde opp blanke ark ble et symbol på sorg og ytring under sensur. Na de brand in Urumqi in november 2022 vonden op veel plaatsen demonstraties plaats tegen het zero-COVID-beleid en voor vrijheid van meningsuiting. Alzare fogli bianchi è diventato un simbolo di lutto e di espressione sotto censura.

事件／專題概述

官方通報稱，烏魯木齊吉祥苑火災造成10人死亡、9人受傷；居民、現場材料和海外報道圍繞長期封控是否影響逃生與救援提出質疑。其後上海烏魯木齊中路和其他城市的集會中，人們以舉起空白紙張表達哀悼、反封控訴求與對審查的抗議。

詳細時間線或關鍵發展

  1. 2022-11-24： 官方通報稱，烏魯木齊吉祥苑火災造成10人死亡、9人受傷；居民和海外報道質疑長期封控是否影響逃生與救援。
  2. 2022-11-26： 上海烏魯木齊中路出現悼念與示威；參與者舉起空白紙張，警方在現場介入。
  3. 2022-11-27： 北京、成都、廣州、武漢等地和多所大學出現集會或聲援；空白紙張成為共享而含義多樣的象徵。
  4. 2022-11-28： 警方在多地增加盤查，參與者與媒體報告手機檢查、帶走和後續聯絡。

人權與法治分析

在長期的新冠病毒控制期間，烏魯木齊發生火災，造成十人死亡、九人受傷，上海、北京、廣州、成都和其他城市的人們拿著白紙聚集在一起哀悼、反對限制和抗議審查制度。警方帶走了參與者，檢查或扣留了手機，隨後聯繫了人們，平臺刪除了記錄，在對救援、行動和公共衛生政策的擔憂中增加了拘留、監視和記憶壓制。當局必須獨立調查火災和封鎖的影響，披露每一次拘留和電話搜查，保存抗議記錄，停止報復，賠償非法傷害，並保護和平集會、言論、隱私和有效補救。

保護審查關聯

參加白紙集會、擁有白紙、口號、電話位置數據、設備搜索、平臺帖子、後來的警方聯繫以及家人或朋友的支持都會創建可識別的抗議記錄。警方撤離、電話檢查、後續詢問、帳戶刪除和國家協調都顯示出追蹤街道以外的分散和平行為並進入個人網路的能力。回國、週年紀念或圖片在國外流傳可能會持續曝光；國家必須停止報復、保護參與者和親屬、刪除非法取得的資料、保留證據並提供獨立的救濟。

未解問題

公開資料仍未完整說明部分參與者被帶走、手機檢查或內容刪除的法律依據、具體程序與後續處置。獨立調查能否安全接觸受影響者、查驗現場材料並取得救濟結果，仍是與火災、抗議和後續追查相關的重要問題。

Detailed timeline and key developments

  1. 2022-11-24： An official notice said the Urumqi Jixiangyuan fire killed 10 people and injured 9; residents and overseas reporting raised questions about whether the prolonged lockdown affected escape and rescue.
  2. 2022-11-26： Mourning and demonstrations took place on Shanghai’s Urumqi Middle Road. Participants held up blank sheets of paper, and police intervened at the scene.
  3. 2022-11-27： Rallies or solidarity events took place in Beijing, Chengdu, Guangzhou, Wuhan, other cities, and many universities. Blank sheets became a shared symbol with varied meanings.
  4. 2022-11-28： The police have increased checks in many places, and participants and the media reported cell phone inspections, removals, and follow-up contacts.
  5. 2022-12-07： The Joint Prevention and Control Mechanism of the State Council announced the “New Ten Articles,” marking a major adjustment to the zero-COVID policy. That policy shift is distinct from the follow-up or procedural risks faced by participants.
  6. 2023-01-26： Human Rights Watch called for the immediate release of everyone detained for participating in the White Paper Protests and for the charges to be dropped.
  7. 2023-11： Around the first anniversary, media and researchers continued to document removal of, or visibility restrictions on, related searches and commemorative content.

Key evidence and information control

On-site images, material uploaded by residents, and overseas reporting preserve records of the gatherings, blank sheets, and police presence. Government notices record policy changes; human-rights organizations and media separately record identity checks, detention, phone checks, and content removal. Each source has a distinct evidentiary role.

Official position, silence and information control

The Xinjiang government fire briefing records the official figures of 10 deaths and 9 injuries; the State Council’s Joint Prevention and Control Mechanism later published adjusted COVID-control measures. Those documents set out official figures and policy. Residents, on-site material, media, and human-rights organizations separately record questions about escape and rescue, the gatherings, police action, and content restrictions; the public record remains incomplete on particular measures and procedures.

Advocacy pressure and state machinery

Police taking participants away, checking or temporarily retaining phones, and platforms removing posts are distinct actions. Public material and reporting record identity checks, phone or account checks, summonses, and later contact after gatherings in several cities; removed or down-ranked material also narrowed public discussion and evidence preservation.

Human rights and rule-of-law analysis

After an Urumqi fire killed ten people and injured nine during prolonged Covid controls, people in Shanghai, Beijing, Guangzhou, Chengdu, and other cities gathered with blank paper to mourn, oppose restrictions, and protest censorship. Police took participants away, checked or retained phones, contacted people later, and platforms removed records, adding detention, surveillance, and memory suppression to concerns about rescue, movement, and public-health policy. Authorities must independently investigate the fire and lockdown effects, disclose every detention and phone search, preserve protest records, stop retaliation, compensate unlawful harm, and protect peaceful assembly, expression, privacy, and effective remedy.

Relevance to protection review

Attendance at a blank-paper gathering, possession of a white sheet, slogans, phone-location data, device searches, platform posts, later police contact, and support from family or friends create identifiable protest records. Police removal, phone inspection, follow-up questioning, account deletion, and national coordination demonstrate capacity to trace a decentralized and peaceful act beyond the street and into personal networks. Return, anniversary commemoration, or circulation of images abroad may sustain exposure; the state must end reprisals, protect participants and relatives, delete unlawfully obtained data, preserve evidence, and provide independent relief.

Unresolved questions

Public material does not fully explain the legal basis, procedures, or subsequent handling of some participants taken away, phone checks, or content removals. Whether an independent inquiry can safely hear affected people, examine on-site material, and obtain outcomes of available remedies remains a central question about the fire, the protests, and the subsequent pursuit.

人権・法の支配の分析

長期にわたる新型コロナウイルス対策の中でウルムチの火災で10人が死亡、9人が負傷した後、上海、北京、広州、成都などの都市の人々が白紙の紙を持って集まり、追悼し、規制に反対し、検閲に抗議した。警察は参加者を連行し、携帯電話を検査または保管し、後で人々に連絡し、プラットフォームは記録を削除し、救助、移動、公衆衛生政策に関する懸念に拘留、監視、記憶抑制を加えた。当局は独自に火災と封鎖の影響を調査し、すべての拘留と電話捜索を公開し、抗議活動の記録を保存し、報復を停止し、不法被害を補償し、平和的な集会、表現、プライバシー、効果的な救済策を保護しなければならない。

保護審査との関連

白紙の集会への参加、白いシートの所持、スローガン、電話位置データ、デバイスの検索、プラットフォームへの投稿、その後の警察からの連絡、家族や友人からの支援などにより、特定可能な抗議記録が作成されます。警察の排除、電話検査、追跡調査、アカウント削除、国家調整は、街頭を越えて個人的なネットワークにまで分散した平和的行為を追跡する能力を証明しています。返品、記念日の記念、または海外での画像の流通により、露出が続く可能性があります。国は報復をやめ、参加者と親族を保護し、不法に入手したデータを削除し、証拠を保全し、独立した救済を提供しなければなりません。

未解決の問題

사건·주제 개요

공식 통보는 우루무치 화재로 10명이 사망하고 9명이 다쳤다고 밝혔다. 주민, 현장 자료와 해외 보도는 장기 봉쇄가 탈출과 구조에 영향을 주었는지 의문을 제기했다. 이후 상하이 우루무치 중로와 다른 도시의 집회에서 사람들은 애도, 봉쇄 반대, 검열 항의를 표현하며 백지를 들었다.

Dopo che un incendio a Urumqi ha ucciso dieci persone e ne ha ferite nove durante i controlli Covid prolungati, le persone a Shanghai, Pechino, Guangzhou, Chengdu e in altre città si sono riunite con carta bianca per piangere, opporsi alle restrizioni e protestare contro la censura. La polizia ha portato via i partecipanti, ha controllato o trattenuto i telefoni, ha contattato le persone in seguito e le piattaforme hanno rimosso i record, aggiungendo detenzione, sorveglianza e soppressione della memoria alle preoccupazioni su salvataggio, movimento e politica di sanità pubblica. Le autorità devono indagare in modo indipendente sugli effetti degli incendi e del lockdown, rivelare ogni detenzione e perquisizione telefonica, preservare i registri delle proteste, fermare le ritorsioni, risarcire i danni illegali e proteggere le riunioni pacifiche, l’espressione, la privacy e i rimedi efficaci.

Rilevanza per l’esame della protezione

La partecipazione a un raduno di carta bianca, il possesso di un foglio bianco, slogan, dati sulla posizione del telefono, ricerche di dispositivi, post sulla piattaforma, successivi contatti con la polizia e il sostegno di familiari o amici creano documenti di protesta identificabili. La rimozione della polizia, l’ispezione telefonica, gli interrogatori successivi, la cancellazione degli account e il coordinamento nazionale dimostrano la capacità di tracciare un atto decentralizzato e pacifico oltre la strada e nelle reti personali. Il ritorno, la commemorazione dell'anniversario o la circolazione delle immagini all'estero possono sostenere l'esposizione; lo Stato deve porre fine alle ritorsioni, proteggere i partecipanti e i parenti, cancellare i dati ottenuti illegalmente, preservare le prove e fornire assistenza indipendente.

Questioni irrisolte

Il materiale pubblico non spiega pienamente base giuridica, procedure o trattamento successivo in alcuni casi di persone portate via, verifiche dei telefoni o contenuti rimossi. Resta centrale stabilire se un’indagine indipendente possa ascoltare in sicurezza le persone coinvolte, esaminare i materiali sul posto e accertare l’esito dei rimedi disponibili.
