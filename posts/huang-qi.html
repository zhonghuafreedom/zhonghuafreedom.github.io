黃琦是中國早期互聯網人權網站「六四天網」創辦人。該網站長期記錄訪民、強拆、冤案和地方維權資訊。2019年，黃琦被以「故意洩露國家秘密」等罪名判處十二年。

時間線

  • 1998年左右，黃琦創辦六四天網，後成為中國重要民間人權資訊平台。
  • 2016年11月，黃琦被拘押。
  • 2019年7月，法院以國家秘密相關罪名判處其十二年徒刑。
  • 國際組織長期關注其健康、探視和程序公正問題。

為何屬於言論自由迫害

黃琦案的核心不只是個人言論，而是獨立人權資訊平台能否存在。當訪民故事、地方冤案和公共資料被包裝成「國家秘密」，民間記錄空間就會被壓縮。

現況與關注

截至2026年7月8日，公開資料顯示黃琦仍受長刑期影響。引用此案時應特別標明官方罪名與人權組織判斷之間的區別，並避免公開家屬敏感聯絡資訊。

Huang Qi founded 64 Tianwang, one of China’s early online human-rights information sites. The site documented petitioners, forced evictions, miscarriages of justice, and local rights cases. In 2019, Huang was sentenced to twelve years on state-secrets related charges.

Timeline

  • Around 1998, Huang founded 64 Tianwang, later a key civic rights information platform.
  • In November 2016, Huang was detained.
  • In July 2019, a court sentenced him to twelve years on state-secrets related charges.
  • International groups have long raised concerns about his health, visitation, and fair-trial rights.

Why this is free-expression persecution

Huang’s case concerns not only individual speech but the existence of an independent human-rights information platform. When stories from petitioners, local injustice, and public records are treated as state secrets, civic documentation is suppressed.

Status and concern

As of July 8, 2026, public sources indicate that Huang remains affected by a long sentence. This site should distinguish official charges from rights-group assessments and avoid publishing sensitive family contact information.
