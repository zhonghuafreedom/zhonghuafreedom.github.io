新疆與西藏問題的共同核心，是國家以「民族團結」「反恐」「扶貧」「雙語教育」等名義，對宗教、語言、家庭、勞動和文化傳承進行深度干預。

強迫勞動與勞動轉移

聯合國專家在2026年再次對涉及維吾爾人、藏人與其他少數民族的國家施加強迫勞動指控表示關切，指出相關模式可能涉及強制轉移或奴役等嚴重國際罪行風險。中國政府否認相關指控。

寄宿學校與語言同化

人權組織持續記錄新疆與西藏兒童被送入以普通話為主的寄宿制教育環境。這類政策通常被官方稱為教育公平或現代化，但批評者認為其削弱家庭傳承、母語、宗教和地方文化。

宗教與文化空間

清真寺、寺院、宗教教育、節慶、服飾、姓名、朝聖、宗教出版和網絡表達都可能受到審查與行政控制。宗教領袖、作家、學者、藝術家和教師尤其容易成為目標。

Xinjiang and Tibet share a central pattern: state intervention in religion, language, family life, labour, and cultural transmission under the language of “ethnic unity,” “counterterrorism,” “poverty alleviation,” or “bilingual education.”

Forced labour and labour transfer

UN experts again raised concerns in 2026 about allegations of state-imposed forced labour involving Uyghur, Tibetan, and other minorities, warning that coercive elements could amount to grave international crimes such as forcible transfer or enslavement. The Chinese government denies the allegations.

Boarding schools and language assimilation

Human rights organizations continue to document Uyghur and Tibetan children being placed in Mandarin-dominant boarding education. Officials describe these policies as education equity or modernization; critics argue that they weaken family transmission, mother tongues, religion, and local culture.

Religious and cultural space

Mosques, monasteries, religious education, festivals, dress, names, pilgrimages, religious publishing, and online expression can be subject to censorship and administrative control. Religious leaders, writers, scholars, artists, and teachers are especially vulnerable.

Website recommendation

The site can separate Xinjiang and Tibet into dedicated pages while keeping a shared-pattern page on forced assimilation, labour transfers, boarding schools, religious control, language policy, and pressure on families across borders. Each claim should link to original reports where possible.
